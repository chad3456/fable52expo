Ramakien

The Ramayana as it is told in Thailand, drawn in a flat inked style that borrows the masks and flame ornament of Thai khon theatre and the paper and lacquer colours of Vietnamese Đông Hồ prints. Eight verses, eighty seconds, a pentatonic score for plucked strings, xylophone, gongs and drums. The music is synthesised; there are no vocals.
